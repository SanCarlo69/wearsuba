Why SUBA?

SUBA

Souvenir- und Bekleidungsartikel

German for souvenir and clothing articles. That is how we started in 2023.

We started with souvenir and clothing articles. Today we make everything from socks and lanyards to mugs that your brand can wear, use and give away. Four letters, many meanings. Which one fits you?

How we work

From idea to finished delivery

  1. Enquiry & advice

    Send us your idea, logo and quantity – we advise you on material and technique. Quote within 24 hours.

  2. Preview & approval

    Depending on the project, you receive a technical drawing, a photo of the sample or a physical sample. Production only starts once you approve.

  3. Production

    After approval, production starts with ongoing quality control and full transparency.

  4. Delivery

    Quality-checked, packed and sent straight to you – or drop-shipped to your customers.
